Meet the two species we studied

Rusty crayfish

We asked how rusty crayfish affect Michigan’s waters and how people can help stop their spread. Rusty crayfish can compete with native crayfish and eat aquatic plants. One action we share is to keep unused bait out of lakes and streams.

See our rusty crayfish research

Asian longhorned tick

We studied what this tick looks like, how it reproduces, and how it affects animals. We learned that females can reproduce without mating and that an expert can help tell this tick apart from others.

See our Asian longhorned tick research
