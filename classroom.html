Michigan invasive species: Classroom guide

Our team used student notebooks, interviews with biologists and a survey of our robotics community to plan an awareness project. Start with the two species we chose, then think about how to teach someone else what you learned.

Rusty crayfish

Our notebook team asked what damage rusty crayfish can cause and how people can help prevent its spread. Rusty crayfish compete with native crayfish and eat aquatic plants. A simple action: keep unused bait out of lakes and streams. Clean, drain and dry boats and gear between waters.

Asian longhorned tick

Our notebook team studied what this tick looks like, how it reproduces and how it affects animals. Females can reproduce without mating, and large numbers can feed on one animal. After time outdoors, check for ticks and ask an adult for help. An expert can confirm the species.

Try it: A 10-minute awareness challenge

  1. Choose one species. Explain one way it affects Michigan in your own words.
  2. Write one action a family can take. Explain how that action helps.
  3. Trade answers with a partner. Can they name the species and repeat the action without looking?

Our team used a QR code on its Hype Chain to start this kind of conversation. Where could you put a message so the right people would see it?
